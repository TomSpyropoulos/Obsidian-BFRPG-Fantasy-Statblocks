A Giant Snail is a great molluscoid creature with a hard shell covering most of its body. It attacks by slamming its body into its opponents, covering them in its mucus (a powerful poison). The victim of this poison must succeed on a DC 13 CON check or be paralyzed for 1 turn.

It leaves a trail of this mucus which is also slippery, and anything attempting to sprint through it must succeed on a DC 13 CON check or fall and be subject to the poison's effects.

If surrounded, the giant snail may retreat into its shell, giving it an amor class of 20 and a hardness of 4 (see the **Stronghold** section in the **Basic** **Fantasy** **RPG** **Core** **Rules**).
