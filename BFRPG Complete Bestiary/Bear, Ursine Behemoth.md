An Ursine Behemoth is a rare bear of truly gargantuan size. Any particular breed of bear might produce an ursine behemoth, as they are born normal-looking but later grow to massive proportions. When standing upright on its hind legs, the ursine behemoth is 20 feet to 30 feet tall. It has a typical temperament for a bear, but due to its massive form it is continuously hungry.

An ursine behemoth attacks like others bears, but is not able to hug opponents who are not at least larger than a humanoid.
