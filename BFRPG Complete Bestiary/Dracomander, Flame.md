A Flame Dracomander looks like a giant snake more than 12 feet long, with a dragon-like head and wings and lizard forelimbs. Its scales are all the colors of flame: red, orange, and yellow. A flame dracomander is flaming hot, and all non-fire-resistant creatures within 20 feet of the monster suffer 1d8 points of damage per round from the heat. It is immune to damage from any fire or heat attack.
