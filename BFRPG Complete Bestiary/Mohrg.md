A Mohrg is the animated corpse of a mass murderer or other heinous villain. The average mohrg is 5-6 feet tall and weighs about 120 pounds, and can easily be confused with a zombie. A mohrg attacks by striking enemies with its fists, but it may also lash out with its 4-foot long tongue. Whomever the tongue touches must succeed on a DC 14 STR check or become paralyzed for 6d4 rounds. Unlike zombies, mohrgs move at full speed and have no penalty to initiative.

As with all undead, they may be Turned by a Cleric (as a vampire), and are immune to sleep, charm, or hold magic. Creatures killed by a mohrg rise after 1d4 days as ordinary zombies under the mohrg's control.
