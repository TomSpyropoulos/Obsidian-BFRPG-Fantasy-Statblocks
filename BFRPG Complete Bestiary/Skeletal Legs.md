Animated from the lower half of a skeleton, Skeletal Legs are often spawned from either mistake or desperation. These necromantic beings, while not as desirable, do have their strengths. Due to their reduced weight, they are quicker and more agile than your usual skeleton and gain surprise on 1-3 on 1d6.
