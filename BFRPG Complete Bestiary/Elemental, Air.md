Air elementals resemble "dust devils," that is, small whirlwinds, but they are much more powerful. Air elementals take double damage when attacked by earth-based attacks (including by earth elementals). An air elemental may choose either to attack a single opponent, thus receiving one attack per round at the listed damage, or may choose to knock all opponents in a 5' radius to the ground; if the latter attack is used, all creatures of 2 levels or less must succeed on a DC 13 CON check or fall prone. Creatures of 3 or more levels or levels are not so affected. Air elementals do an additional 1d8 points of damage against creatures or vehicles which are airborne.
