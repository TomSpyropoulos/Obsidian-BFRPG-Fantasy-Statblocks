Like all nagas, Aquatic Nagas have long, snake-like bodies covered with glistening scales and more or less human heads and faces. Their skin color ranges from an almost metallic blue through turquoise to emerald green, patterned in a reticulated fashion. They are the smallest nagas, being around 10 feet in length and weighing around 200 pounds.

In combat, aquatic nagas usually stay mostly concealed in a body of water from where they launch a spell attack, but in close combat they will not hesitate to use their deadly venomous bite (if bitten, Succeed on a DC 13 CON check or die). However, they are not naturally aggressive and will often parley with characters. Each has the spell-casting abilities of a 5th level Wizard.
