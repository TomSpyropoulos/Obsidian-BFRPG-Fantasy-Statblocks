A Crawling Claw (or Animated Hand) is a type of undead creature created from a severed hand. They are used for many tasks, from menial chores to discrete killings. Crawling claws are a popular tool used by necromancers. Due to their size and quickness, they are able to hide effectively, with only a 30% chance of detection.
