Dancing Monitors are a large variety of lizards that secrete an oil on their skin that facilitates the delivery of poison and venom. When they are agitated, they stand up on their hind legs and whirl and flail about. As they dance, they strike their victims and transfer the oil from their skin, and then they bite their victims to deliver a lethal dose of venom.

Dancing monitors will first make at least one successful touch attack against their victims, which applies a save-targeting penalty on DC 12 CON check. Once a target's fortitude is softened enough, they go in for the kill with a venomous bite which requires a DC 12 CON check to avoid instant death. The oil may be cleaned off with soap and water, although this is normally impossible in the middle of combat. Otherwise, the oil will absorb into the victim's skin and remain in effect for 24 hours.
