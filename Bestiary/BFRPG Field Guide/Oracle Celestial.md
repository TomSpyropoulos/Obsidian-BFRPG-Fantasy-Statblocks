Oracles are considered the wisest members of the celestial hierarchy, possesing as they do the ability to see beyond the present into the future. With this gift they can unravel mysteries that are often hidden from mortals and even other celestial beings.

Known for their jet black skin, and hair of crackling energy, and the two pairs of large, feathered wings, oracles have a heightened sense of danger and can 'taste' deception, combined with their ability to see the future, this makes them especially difficult to defeat. Their telepathic abilities allow them to communicate with any creatures within 100 feet without the need for any verbal communication.
