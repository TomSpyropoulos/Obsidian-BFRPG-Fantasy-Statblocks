An Octopus Fungi appears similar to other giant fungi such as wailing morels (shriekers). If anyone tries to pass an octopus fungi, it will "unfurl" into a single long tentacle and try to wrap around the victim and drag them back to the biting core. A normal attack roll is made; a hit deals 1d6 points of damage from the clawed grasping sucker and a victim must succeed on a DC 13 DEX check, or be drug back to the core the next round. If pulled into the body, two attacks will be made (the tentacle and the mouth) with a +4 bonus each.
