If disturbed, a patch of this mold bursts forth with a cloud of poisonous spores. Each patch covers from 10 to 25 square feet; several patches may grow adjacent to each other, and will appear to be a single patch in this case. Each patch can emit a cloud of spores once per day. All within 10 feet of the mold will be affected by the spores and must succeed on a DC 12 CON check or take 1d8 points of damage per round for 6 rounds. Sunlight renders yellow mold dormant.
